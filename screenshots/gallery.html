Toast data governance proof of concept in DataHub

Toast's 17 public APIs cataloged as 60 tables and 3,281 columns in seven business subdomains. Every column carries a classification tier and, where it holds personal or card data, privacy tags that say what it is and whose it is. About 200 glossary terms (KPIs from Toast's SEC filings, the Toast Platform Guide glossary and Toast's API objects) are linked to the tables and columns they describe, with lineage to an illustrative KPI model, classification-driven access policies, certification workflows and automated governance monitoring.

Independent project, not affiliated with Toast. The KPI model and policy documents are illustrative. Asset links open the live DataHub Cloud instance, which needs a login: request read-only access.

Built with DataHub Cloud, Toast's public OpenAPI specifications and the acryl-datahub Python SDK.
